Five directions for awethon.github.io

Homepage only — that's the part you disliked. All centred, all low-text, no orange. Open each and hover things.

Concept 1

Console

PaperMod's dryness, rebuilt. Monospace metadata, ice-blue accent, posts as a tight dated index rather than cards. The most neutral of the five.

Concept 2

Glass Panel

The Raycast read. Floating pill header, one frosted panel for the bio, glass cards for posts, cool blue-grey light behind it. No hue in the accent — just light.

Concept 3

Editorial Quiet

Light, paper-warm, Newsreader serif set large and light-weight. Slate accent. Closest to “dry and neutral” without any UI chrome at all.

lightserifquietest
Concept 4

Soft Desk

The duckquill register. Rounded cards on a light grey desk, pill header, soft slate-teal accent, a small “Currently” callout. Cute but sober.

Concept 5

Aurora

The Linear read. Slow-drifting blue/teal mesh behind a flat neutral foreground, gradient-clipped headline, posts as one hairline-divided glass table.

darklinearboldest

Round two

Console × Aurora. No boxes, floating chrome only, working light/dark toggle in each header — check both.

Concept 6

Ledger

Console with a system: numbered entries, tabular dates, a rule that draws itself across the row on hover. The header is a floating capsule, nothing else is a box.

Concept 7

Atmosphere

Aurora with the AI tells removed — no status pill, no gradient text, no blobs. A single directional sky wash, and hairline margin rules running the full height of the page as the motif.

Concept 8

Slash

Everything built from the one element you liked. The mark is the hero, nav reads /posts /tags /rss, and each entry reveals its own path on hover.

Round three

Four replacements for Aurora's backdrop. Identical foreground and content in all four — only the sky changes, so the comparison is fair. All four also fix the dead .grain layer that concept 5 never styled.

Concept 9

Rays

Light shafts instead of curtains. Nine soft beams fanning from a point just above the header, blurred and slowly breathing. Nine layers instead of thirteen, narrower, so it composites far cheaper than 5 does.

CSS onlycheapestmost restrained
Concept 10

Halftone

Aurora's exact geometry, pushed through a 7px dot grid. Soft dots, so bright areas spread and dim ones shrink to nothing — print/plotter rather than lens flare, which sits better next to the mono type.

CSS onlyprint textureclosest to 5
Concept 11

ASCII

The same curtain field, sampled onto a monospace grid and drawn as .:-=+*#%@ in a single <pre>, coloured by one gradient clipped to the glyphs. Repaints at 14fps on purpose — it should read as a terminal, not a screensaver.

~90 lines JSmost distinctiveties to Console
Concept 12

Shader

What 5 is approximating by hand, done properly: one canvas, one fragment shader, three noise-warped curtains plus stars. Renders at 1280px wide and 30fps, falls back to a CSS sky with no WebGL or with reduced motion.
